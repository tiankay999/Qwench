# Qwench: read this first

Qwench is Ghana's fraud bureau. Its first product is Know Your Payee: before a
payment goes, a bank or MoMo app checks the recipient against schemes regulators
have named and fraud institutions have confirmed.

Before changing anything, read [docs/spec/README.md](docs/spec/README.md). It gives
the reading order, who owns what, and where the code already differs from the spec.
